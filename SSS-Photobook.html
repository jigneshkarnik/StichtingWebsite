Our Mission

Stichting Sanskriti & Sanskar Foundation is a registered cultural bridge dedicated to uniting, reviving, and helping thrive the Culture, Values, Customs, Heritage, and Traditions of both India and The Netherlands. We connect generations through art, literature, and stories.
